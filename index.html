    Before you go

    The quick answers about Manana Mex breakfast.

    What time is breakfast served?

    From 6:00am to 10:30am at every restaurant, every day of the week. After 10:30 the kitchen switches to the lunch menu.

    Can I order the night before?

    Yes. Order in the app or on this site any time and choose a pickup slot for the next morning. Your order is made fresh at the time you pick, not the night before.

    How fast is it?

    Every burrito is made to order. If you order ahead, it's waiting on the pickup shelf at your time, so you don't queue at all. At the drive-thru, give your name at the speaker and drive straight to the window.

    Is there something under $10?

    The Mini Manana is $5.90, and the Uni Saver combo gets you a Mini, a hash brown and a flat white for $9.90.

    Do you have vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free options?

    The Green Light burrito and Mini Manana are vegetarian. The Tofu Ranchero Tacos and hash brown are vegan. The Sunrise Bowl and both tacos are made without gluten ingredients, but our kitchens do handle gluten.

    Which restaurants are open 24/7?

    31 of our restaurants never close. Use the Open 24/7 filter on the Find us page to see the ones near you. Breakfast is still served from 6:00am there.
